Calibrated on [2018, 2019, 2020, 2021, 2022, 2023]; evaluated on held-out [2024, 2025].

Confidence means the historical rate of average PPR over the next four calendar weeks falling within ±5 points per scheduled team game of the forecast. It does not mean exact rank correctness. Calibration groups include position, season stage, projected scoring level and recent activity, falling back to broader groups when fewer than 150 forecast windows are available.

86,404 calibration windows; 26,836 held-out windows. These overlap and repeat players.
